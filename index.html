Capture and hold Royal Stables. Burn enemy TCs near Stables when safe.
Group B — Strong Player 2 + 4
Capture and hold Bell Tower. Burn enemy TCs near Bell Tower when safe.

Capture and hold nearest Sanctum. Reinforce priority buildings as needed.
Group D — 5 players
Capture and hold nearest Abbey. At 15:00, assist with Reformation Hall if secure.

HealingSave healing speedups for important defenses and retakes.
TeleportsUse free teleports strategically. Royal Stables reduces cooldown.
SpeedupsSave march speedups for urgent captures and reinforcements.
Enemy TCsBurn enemies landing near Stables or Bell Tower without abandoning garrisons.
Reformation HallPrioritize its +15% damage and −15% damage-taken bonuses at 15:00.
